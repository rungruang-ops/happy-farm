# Happy Farm — MVP acceptance specification

## Product
- Audience: children aged 2–5, ideally supported by a caregiver.
- Lead character: Bryan the Piglet; yellow T-shirt, red overalls.
- Visual target: bright, friendly, rounded 3D animated farm world, following the user-supplied design reference.
- Interaction: large, forgiving touch targets; simple one-step prompts; encouraging feedback; no punishment for wrong answers.

## Activities
1. Animal Friends — identify the requested animal.
2. Farm Colors — identify the requested color.
3. Garden Time — identify the requested produce.
4. Feed the Animals — give the requested food to the animal.
5. Count the Eggs — tap three eggs.

## Acceptance gates
- A child can reach an activity from the home screen without typing.
- All activities provide immediate positive feedback and can be completed with touch input.
- Progress and stars persist locally after reload.
- Layout is usable at mobile and desktop widths.
- Final art is compared against the supplied reference before sign-off; the current SVG/CSS visuals are placeholders and do not pass the final 3D-art gate.
- No deploy, paid service, tracking, or external API without explicit approval.
